# Subpower index of primitive local generators for a disjoint-core profile

Fix `n,d>=2`, put `m=nd`, and let `(X_i,Y_i) in Z^2`, `1<=i<=m`,
be nonzero binary rows with every bracket

```text
Delta_ij = X_i Y_j-X_j Y_i != 0.
```

For each nonempty coalition `T subset [m]`, let `n_T` be a positive
integer. Assume the nonunit `n_T` have pairwise disjoint rational-prime
support and that

```text
Delta_ij = t_ij product_(T containing i,j) n_T,
               t_ij in Z\{0}.                              (1)
```

The `n_T` may equal one. Set

```text
g_i=gcd(|X_i|,|Y_i|),
B=product_(i<j)|t_ij|,       Gamma=product_i g_i.
```

Let `M_Z` be the integral multilinear `SL_n` invariant lattice for
`m` vector rows. For every `2n`-set `I` and every complementary
determinant-product invariant `H`, form

```text
G_(I,H)=det[(X_i V_i | Y_i V_i)_(i in I)] H.
```

Divide each nonzero `G_(I,H)` by the positive gcd of its integer
monomial coefficients, and let `N_prim` be the lattice generated by
these primitive columns. Let `K_Z` be the saturated coherent kernel
for the distinct projective directions `(X_i:Y_i)`.
Because `H` uses complementary rows and has coefficient content one,
this is equivalently `(L_I/content(L_I)) H`, the usual primitive
local column. Distinct-configuration determinant generation gives
`N_prim tensor Q=K_Z tensor Q`, so the index below is finite.

**Theorem.** With `A=A_(n,d)` and `H=H_(n,d)` from the
[residual-discriminant local theorem](residual_discriminant_local_index_bound.md),

```text
[K_Z:N_prim] divides A B^H Gamma^(H m(m-1)).               (2)
```

The constants depend only on `n,d`; they do not depend on the sizes
or valuations of the core factors `n_T`. If `Y_i!=0` for every `i`,
then `Gamma<=product_i |Y_i|`. Consequently, for a fixed `n,d`
family with

```text
log B=o(w),       sum_i log|Y_i|=o(w),                       (3)
```

one has `log[K_Z:N_prim]=o(w)`. The
[full Boolean small-imaginary profile](full_boolean_small_imaginary_circuits.md)
has (1) and (3) under its stated core hypotheses, so this is an
index statement for its actual extracted binary rows, conditional on
those hypotheses. It bounds the index incurred when joining supports,
one of the issues identified in the
[support-family diagnostic](local_generator_support_family_diagnostic.md).

For the actual anchor numerators in
[the central truncation reduction](endpoint_central_truncation.md),
`P_i=X_i+iY_i` is coprime to its Gaussian conjugate. Hence
`gcd(X_i,Y_i)=1` and the coordinates have opposite parity. In this
application `Gamma=1` exactly, so (2) simplifies to
`[K_Z:N_prim] | A B^H`. This removes a row-content correction from
the existing subpower index bound; it does not improve the available
Archimedean first-minimum estimate or the general growth rate.

## Local chart at a rational prime

Fix a rational prime `p`, and put `c_i=v_p(g_i)`, `u=max_i c_i`.
Work over a finite unramified extension `R/Z_p` whose residue field
has more than `m+1` elements. Its uniformizer is still `p`, and
passing to `R` leaves the Smith exponents of an integer matrix
unchanged. The primitive binary rows

```text
r_i=(X_i/g_i,Y_i/g_i)
```

are nonzero modulo `p`. A covector `ell` can be chosen over `R`
with `ell(r_i)` a unit for every `i`: at most `m` projective covectors
are excluded modulo `p`.

If `p` divides no `n_T`, choose another covector `f`, independent of
`ell`, with `f(r_i)` a unit for every `i`; the residue-field size
ensures that two such covectors exist. Put `z_i=f(r_i)/ell(r_i)`.
The change of binary basis has unit determinant, and all `z_i` are
units. Moreover

```text
v_p(z_i-z_j)=v_p(Delta_ij)-c_i-c_j
            =v_p(t_ij)-c_i-c_j.                             (4)
```

Use the empty coalition in the local theorem. Its residual
discriminant has valuation

```text
v_p(D_empty)=sum_(i<j)v_p(z_i-z_j)
             <= sum_(i<j)v_p(t_ij).                          (5)
```

Suppose instead that `p` divides the unique core `n_T`, and write
`e=v_p(n_T)`. Choose `i_0 in T`. Take a primitive covector `f` to
vanish on `r_(i_0)` and choose `ell` as before; their reductions are
independent because
`ell(r_(i_0))` is a unit. Then `z_(i_0)=0`, and the unit binary
change again gives

```text
v_p(z_i-z_j)=e 1_(i,j in T)+v_p(t_ij)-c_i-c_j.             (6)
```

Put `e'=max(0,e-2u)` and `s=p^e'`, allowing `s=1`. Equation (6)
implies `z_i in sR` for every `i in T`. Write `z_i=s a_i` there
and `z_j=b_j` outside. All factors of the local residual
discriminant are nonzero because the original brackets are nonzero.
The factors within `T` have valuations

```text
v_p(a_i-a_k)=e+v_p(t_ik)-c_i-c_k-e'
             <=v_p(t_ik)+2u.                                (7)
```

The factors `b_j-b_l` outside `T` and `b_j=z_j-z_(i_0)` for
`j outside T` have valuations at most `v_p(t_jl)` and
`v_p(t_(i_0)j)`, respectively. These three sets of charged pairs
are disjoint. Therefore

```text
v_p(D_T(a,b))
 <= sum_(i<j)v_p(t_ij)+2 binom(|T|,2)u
 <= v_p(B)+m(m-1) sum_i c_i.                                (8)
```

The residual-discriminant theorem applies even when `e'=0`: its
universal maximal-minor identity can be specialized at the unit
`s=1`.

## Primitive columns contain the local normalized lattice

For a support `I`, let `g_I=max(0,|I cap T|-n)` at a core prime,
and take `g_I=0` at a noncore prime. The unit binary change and the
unit denominators `ell(r_i)` give, for each complementary invariant
`H`, an identity of vector-row polynomials over `R`:

```text
G_(I,H) = unit * p^(sum_(i in I)c_i+e' g_I)
                   * (normalized local generator_(I,H)).    (9)
```

The normalized generator has coefficients in `R`. Hence the
integer coefficient gcd of `G_(I,H)` has `p`-valuation at least the
exponent displayed in (9). Dividing by that *actual* gcd only makes
the resulting primitive column larger as an `R`-lattice generator.
Thus the lattice `N_local` of all normalized local generators
satisfies

```text
N_local subset N_prim tensor_Z R subset K_Z tensor_Z R.    (10)
```

This comparison incurs no additional power of `Gamma`.

Let `delta_p` be the `p`-adic length of `K_Z/N_prim`. Unramified
base change preserves it. Equations (5), (8), (10), and the local
theorem give, for every `p`,

```text
delta_p <= v_p(A)+H v_p(B)+H m(m-1) v_p(Gamma).             (11)
```

Only finitely many right-hand sides are nonzero. Summing (11) over
primes and using `[K_Z:N_prim]=product_p p^delta_p` proves the
divisibility (2), and (3) gives its subpower consequence.

## What the index does and does not measure

The argument uses the gcd of *all* maximal minors of the local
generator matrix through its Smith exponents. It shows that
primitivizing individual columns and joining all supports introduces
only subpower index under (3). It does not supply an Archimedean
covolume lower bound for `N_prim` or `K_Z`. In particular, one
large minor of a redundant column family cannot lower-bound that
covolume; the full maximal-minor gcd is the relevant integral datum.
