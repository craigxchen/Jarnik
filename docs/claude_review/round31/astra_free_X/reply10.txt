WHERE THIS LEAVES THE RATE

The order-s barrier is a worst-case phenomenon, not a height phenomenon: at the smooth critical height typical parents have O(log s) points, very probably O(1). Everything the fakes imitate is local or character-tested. What they do not have is (T1): for EVERY pair of a genuine cluster the integer Q_ij = prod_{p in D_ij} p is a sum x^2 + t^2 with t = t_ij tiny (|t_ij| <= (C/2) sqrt(Q_ij)/q^{1/4}, mean log|t_ij| = O(log M) when W = O(M log M)), the difference sets D_ij are closed under symmetric difference along triangles, and the Gaussian integers X_ij multiply accordingly. In an angle model Im X_ij is a real number; integrality of all binom(M,2) of them is the genuine input, and so far it has been used only through the entropy of a random parent.

THE TENTH QUESTION

Work on ONE fixed parent. Choose (A) or (B), say why, and do one of them properly.

(A) THE COCYCLE ON A FIXED PARENT. Take squarefree N' with s split primes of comparable size (all in [P, 2P], P <= s^A), or bounded exponents if you prefer. Either
  (A1) prove M = o(s) for every such parent, or for one explicit dense family where no randomness is available: N' = the product of ALL split primes in [P, 2P], or of all split primes up to X (the profile extremal for Theorem 1.1; a rate there would already be a theorem about an explicit sequence of circles). Many triangles share blocks: a cluster of M marks gives binom(M,2) near-square subproducts and binom(M,3) identities on one prime set; (T2) uses one. Or
  (A2) build the obstruction: an "integral-cofactor fake", i.e. data on a Legendre or other profile with M >> s in which the reduced chords are genuine Gaussian integers satisfying g_ij b_ij + g_jk b_jk + g_ki b_ki = 0 with the exact divisibilities, and as many further genuine properties as you can keep (state exactly which is dropped: the exact norm, the arc, the unit class). If such a fake exists the cocycle is not the missing input either; if you can prove none exists on the Legendre profile, say what the proof uses. The twin relaxation (T5) drops the signed residues; do not drop those.
(B) P3-ALL* (the judge's first choice for this thread). Either a factored-residue fake passing e-all at cutoffs 3M^A, A chosen after the weight constant, at W = O(M log M) (minimise the witness heights over right inverses U, a closest-vector problem; the referee's finite-cutoff lifts r_j = pi_j gamma/conj(gamma) are available), or a height-quantified finite-cutoff version of IX.7 (for Norm gamma <= H, the least cutoff X(H) at which ray tests force gamma/conj(gamma) = +-1) followed by a lower bound W >= f(M) M log M, f -> infinity, for witnesses that pass. The judge's advice: if this returns another fake, the residue route is closed for internal work.
