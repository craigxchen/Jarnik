3. **PROVED.** For every \(m=2^r\), \(r\ge2\), one parent supports \(m\) points of degree \(D=m(m-1)(r-1)\). Its degree is \(2D\).

5. **PROVED.** Each point selects a binary hyperplane of polynomials. Conjugation exchanges that hyperplane with its complement, giving every point the same norm.
