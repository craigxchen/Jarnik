REFEREE RESULTS ON PART VI, AND A SEVENTH QUESTION

Six referees and a judge went through Part VI. No error was found in anything you claimed, and nothing is withdrawn. The judge's full report is in this directory as referee_report_VI.md; read it before continuing (sections 1, 3 and 5). The points you can build on:

1. VI.4 is recorded as a theorem for every p = 3 mod 4. Two referees checked it line by line; the four-mark instance (D = 6981) was reproduced three times with independent arithmetic. Corrections and additions:
   - Rate as proved: M_p(D,0) >= ((1/2) log_2 p - o(1)) log D/loglog D. The constant log_2 p (with L ~ (k-1)(n-h)/(g-h), q >= 2^m) needs a separate distinctness check below L = (k-1)(n-g).
   - Padding: multiplying all points by a common monic e keeps c = 0 while deg e <= D - 2 maxdiff, and the slack grows linearly in L, so M_p(D,0) >= m for EVERY D >= D_1(m).
   - Your five-mark certificate is correct but far from minimal. For k >= 3 the full gcd has about 17,500 of extra degree at the F_{q^2}-rational places (fibres of v -> v(alpha) have q^{k-2} elements). Your forms already work at L = 37 (D = 662,632). Forms x_2, x_1, x_1+x_2, x_0, x_0+x_1-x_2 with signs (+,+,-,+,+) give g = 1379 and five points at D = 568,386 (L = 32). Six points occur with q = 27 (g = 833 > h = 729 although q < 2^5), certified with the formal G at D = 6,880,250.
   - Structure of N. In the four-mark example 338 of the 353 primes are squared. For k >= 3 the multiplicities at F_{q^2}-rational places are about q^{k-2}, unbounded in m, and this is forced for every specialisation X = T(t) of this V. 99.3% of deg N sits on primes whose residue field contains F_{q^2}. The degree drop in (VI.5) needs the doubled exponents; the {0,1} half-system version loses it.
   - p = 1 mod 4: the mechanism fails formally. Every half-system S of F_q^* has delta_S >= (q-1)/4, which forces g >= n/2 + h/2, while two kernels give g <= n/2 + h/(2q). Open.
   - Why it does not port. Your transfer paragraph names Frobenius descent. The referee disagrees: descent has an integer analogue (Galois descent from O_K[i]). The one step without a characteristic-0 counterpart is that ONE parent P_V = A_j^q - c_j^{q-1} A_j has a same-degree right composition factor for every kernel. Lemma 1 (Ritt/Engstrom type): if char k does not divide deg f and f(A) = f'(A') with deg A = deg A', then A' = alpha A + beta (take the polynomial part of the Laurent root (P/c)^{1/d}). Every integer analogue computed (one or several characters, digit-vector port, sign templates, number-field roots) has width exponent 0.94-1.00 against the 1/2 needed, or reduces to a one-parameter polynomial template, which your Part IV bounds by 24.
   - The record's growth bound transfers to F_3[t] with A^2+B^2: W >= 3M log_3 M - O(M). Your ternary clusters sit exponentially above it.
   - Every 5-subtuple of the ternary clusters is balanced (Weil), so the F_9(t)/F_3(t) analogues of A_5(delta < 1/30) and (L_5^o) are false as well.

2. VI.5 is recorded as a theorem. Verified to m = 64; the threshold L >= 4m(d-2) is exact for your V (L = 15 fails at m = 4). "Sharp" holds within complete Hadamard cores only.

3. VI.1 is correct. Sharpening: ord(R'/R) is even, so the weight is >= 2 ceil((K+1)/2) + deg H'. It also excludes every fully flipped Hadamard core and single Paley cores (q = 3 mod 8 from q = 11, q = 7 mod 8 from q = 311). It has NO integer analogue: z_1 = gamma^2 (2+i), z_0 = conj(z_1), gamma = x+iy with (x+2y)^2 - 5y^2 = +-1 has |z_1 - z_0| = 2 and parity support {5} while log N -> infinity. Under abc over Q(i) the honest analogue is a Z-support inequality, which is the record's Lang-Waldschmidt support test; the squarefree skew double core passes it.

4. VI.2 and VI.3 are correct. Hypotheses actually used in VI.2: the sign matrix plus the all-ones column is Hadamard; m >= 4; the f_v monic, nonconstant, distinct, f_v != conj f_v; block products critical. Corollary: no complete Hadamard core of order not a power of 2 over F_2[t]. Scope: with k <= m/2 columns the method gives only D >~ k log k, so the quadratic cost comes from completeness. VI.3 is asymptotic with constant about 1/384 and needs all Q/2 forms. New data: a nonconstant translation B beats B = 1 by a factor about 0.7: 8 points at point degree 68 (B = t^2+t, Y = t^11 + theta(t^2+t)) against 104; 16 at 472 (B = t^3+t, T = t^33); 32 at 2607 (B = t^4+t^2, T = t^86). Every winning B is divisible by t and t+1, the escape VI.3 leaves.

5. The filter now reads: no argument valid verbatim over F_2[t] (all N, or squarefree N) or over F_p[t], p = 3 mod 4, with A^2+B^2 (all N) proves the uniform bound. What a proof over Z can still use: characteristic 0 in an essential way; archimedean order; transcendence input; squarefree or bounded-multiplicity N in odd characteristic; ramification; no coefficient Frobenius. For the integer problem nothing has changed.

SEVENTH QUESTION. Three targets, ranked by what they would do for the integer problem. Take the one you judge most promising first and say why.

(a) SQUAREFREE OR BOUNDED-MULTIPLICITY N IN ODD CHARACTERISTIC. Over F_3[t] with A^2+B^2: is sup_D M_3^{sf}(D,0) finite, where N ranges over squarefree polynomials (or over N with all prime multiplicities <= e_0)? Over Z the case "n squarefree" (or n with bounded exponents) of x^2+y^2 = n is a legitimate and already unsolved case of the conjecture, so:
   - a boundedness proof here would be the first mechanism-level statement that survives every filter and is Z[i]-shaped. Say which step uses odd characteristic (it must fail in characteristic 2 by VI.5) and which uses squarefreeness (it must fail for general N by VI.4), and whether each step has an integer form;
   - a construction would close the last algebraic escape. The referee suggests odd sign functions on V (half-systems) whose products drop about n/4 in degree between different kernels, but knows of no mechanism other than the doubled exponents.
   An exhaustive table of M_3^{sf}(D,0) for D up to 12-14 would help either way.

(b) GROWTH SCALE OVER F_2[t]. Still open: 3M log_2 M - O(M) <= W(M) <= 2M^2 log_2 M, empirically about M^2 log_2 M with B != 1. Either construct the case VI.1 still permits (actual polynomials for two Walsh cores on x and x^3 at e = 4d-2, or any family with W = M^{2-c}), or prove W(M) >= omega(M) M log M. In a proof, mark every step that uses characteristic 2, the ultrametric at infinity or the function field; a proof whose steps all exist over Z improves the integer growth rate. The open classes are incomplete Walsh cores, sparse hyperplane subfamilies with B divisible by the small inert primes, non-hyperplane transversals, independent per-prime choices, unions of designs.

(c) A RAMIFIED MODEL. F_3(t)(sqrt(d(t))) with deg d even, nonsquare leading coefficient and ramified finite primes, so that conjugation is not a coefficient Frobenius (Q(i)/Q ramifies at 2; constant extensions ramify nowhere). Does any linearised-polynomial identity survive a non-constant conjugation? Small exact tables of M(D,0) first.

Independently of (a)-(c): characteristic 0 enters your Part IV through Newton linearisation and BMY, and enters the port analysis through uniqueness of same-degree right composition factors. If you see an arithmetic form of either that says anything about integer clusters, however weak, pursue that instead and say so.
