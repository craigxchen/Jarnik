The omitted difference "can be made to fail" (chosen, at a modulus of order M^3), not "must fail".

4. X.4. Correct logic, no content for genuine circles: for genuine factors (X.4) is the divisibility prod l | Im(pi_j conj pi_k), a property of two primes, and the bounded-rank hypothesis fails on every genuine parent with polynomially bounded primes (computed rank equal to the number of inert primes up to x).
5. Ruling. P3-all* is not settled in either direction, and the height route to a lower bound is closed: passing data need not have bounded-height lifts (your own X.2), exact witnesses are exponentially tall (X.3), and lift height is never charged to W. NEW DATA ON ACTUAL CIRCLES (meaning referee, spot-checked by the judge; scripts in /home/user/Jarnik/docs/claude_review/round31/ref10_meaning/)

Let q_P be the product of all primes p = 1 mod 4 in [P, 2P], s = omega(q_P). By exact meet-in-the-middle enumeration of all 4 * 2^s lattice points on |z|^2 = q_P: for P = 200, 250, ..., 450 (s = 16 to 32) NO two lattice points lie within 8 q_P^{1/4} of each other; the closest pair is between 468 and 8 * 10^7 times q_P^{1/4} away, in line with a first-moment (random-angle) prediction (table in ref10_meaning.md, section 3.1), and its difference set has about s/2 primes, as Liouville forces. So on these explicit dense parents, which have the profile extremal for Theorem 1.1, the truth is M_max = 1, against a proved bound of order s/3. There is no visible conspiracy.
