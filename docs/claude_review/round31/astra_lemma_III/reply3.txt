The growth problem supplies something different: at one parent, >> m^5 balanced fifteen-cut five-tuples with cofactors of total log-size O(log m), whose block norms are products of a common set of atoms (see ../free/answer.md Part II.4 and the paragraph "sufficient five-point hypothesis at index one alone"). In other words: for FIXED cofactors t_ij, what is the Zariski closure of the set of block-norm vectors (n_ij) of genuine ten-pair tuples with those cofactors? Equation (II.5) and (II.6) give explicit equations in the n's with the s_ij eliminated. If that closure is a proper subvariety of bounded degree, uniformly in t up to scaling, then the number of five-tuples at one parent with given small cofactors is controlled by Schwartz-Zippel and one gets a growth statement for ten-pair-type sub-configurations.
