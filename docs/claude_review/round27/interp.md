## 2. The coordinator's observation, verified and corrected

The observation has three parts. (i) `Z in Bs(V(A,m'))` iff `k -> Z^k` is a polynomial of degree `< m'` on `A`: this
is `ze.md` Lemma 4.1 (record); for `V_H` only "`<=`" holds (`Z^k|_A in V_H^perp`, a larger space). (ii) A cube form
of dimension `t` supported in a set `S` lies in `V(S,t)` and is nonzero at nondegenerate `Z`: **correct, with the
right coordinates**. (iii) "If cube forms were
certified the zero estimate would be immediate; presumably their cost exceeds the order": **correct, and exactly
critical**; moreover the obstruction is not the cost alone but that such forms do not fit in `A` at all.

### 2.1 Cube (zonotope) forms: correct, and exactly critical

**(ii) made precise.** Let `a_0 in Z^d` and `v_1..v_t in Z^d \ 0` (in `k'`-coordinates; the coordinate step `e_x` of
`k'` is the character `e_x - e_n` of `Z^n_0`). The zonotope form is
`c = prod_i (T_{v_i} - 1) delta_{a_0}`, i.e. `c = sum_{S subset [t]} (-1)^{t-|S|} delta_{a_0 + sum_{i in S} v_i}`
(coefficients add when sums coincide), and

```text
P_c(Y) = Y^{a_0} prod_{i=1}^t (Y^{v_i} - 1),        ord_e(P_c) = t,        c in V(supp c, t).            (2.1)
```

For the actual point, `Z^v != 1` for every `v = e_x - e_y` and `v = e_x` (distinct points); for general `v != 0` it
holds on full-rank clusters (`ze.md` Prop 5.1, record). So `P_c(Z) != 0`. Hence: **if some zonotope form of order
`>= m'` were supported in `A` (with height `<= H`), rigidity would give `P_c(Z) = 0`, a contradiction, and route 5
would close immediately.** This is a special case of the general mechanism of §3 (a chain of single shifts).

**(iii) The cost is exactly critical.** For a zonotope form, `h_{supp c}(beta) = sum_i |<v_i,beta>|` (the support
contains the vertices of the zonotope), so

```text
Lambda(c) = sum_i Lambda(v_i) = (2/W) sum_i X_{v_i}.                                                  (2.2)
```

**Lemma 2.1 (character inequality; proved; the Gaussian gcd form of [D] Prop 5.3(iii)).** For `v in Z^n_0` with
`Z^v != 1`: `|Z^v - 1| >= e^{-X_v/2}`; hence `X_v >= W/2 - 2 ln(|v'|_1 C)` and
`Lambda(v) >= 1 - 4 ln(|v'|_1 C')/W`.

*Proof.* Put `alpha = prod_{v_x>0} z_x^{v_x}`, `beta = prod_{v_x<0} z_x^{-v_x}`, `s = sum v_x^+`; `|alpha| = |beta| = N^{s/2}`
and `Z^v = alpha/beta`. With `g = gcd(alpha,beta)`: at a split `p`, `v_pi(alpha) - v_pi(beta) = <v,a_p>` and
`v_{conj pi}(alpha) = s e_p - v_pi(alpha)` (same for `beta`), so `v_pi(g) + v_{conj pi}(g) = s e_p - |<v,a_p>|`; inert
primes and `1+i` divide both fully. So `N(g) = N^s e^{-X_v}`, and `alpha != beta` gives
`|Z^v - 1| = |alpha - beta|/|beta| >= |g|/N^{s/2} = e^{-X_v/2}`. Upper bound: `|Z^v - 1| <= |<v',u>| <= |v'|_1 C e^{-W/4}`.
Finally `Lambda(v) = sum_p (e_p log p/W)(2|<v,a_p>|/e_p) = 2X_v/W`. QED

So for a zonotope form supported in the ternary cube (then `sum_i |v_{i,x}| <= 2` in each coordinate, so
`|v_i'|_1 <= 2d`) with all `Z^{v_i} != 1`: `Lambda(c) >= t (1 - 4 ln(2dC')/W)`, **cost = order up to a factor
`1 - O(ln(dC')/W)`**. Such a form is never certified: (1.1) would force `P_c(Z) = 0`, while `P_c(Z) =
Z^{a_0} prod (Z^{v_i} - 1) != 0`. Its Liouville lower bound `prod_i e^{-X_{v_i}/2}` is the product of the character
bounds of Lemma 2.1, each attained up to the pair/character cofactor: **zonotope forms are Liouville-tight** (Lemma
K1's phenomenon for products).

**Fitting in `A`.** For a full-rank cluster (all `Z^v != 1`, `v != 0`), or under the hypothesis (CG) of Thm 2.2: if a
zonotope form of order `t` is supported in `A`, then
`t (1 - 4 ln(2dC')/W) <= Lambda(c) <= Lambda(A) <= 2w`, so `t <= 2w (1 + O(ln(dC')/W)) < 4w <= m'` once
`W > 8 ln(2dC')`. **No ±1 cube/zonotope form of order `>= m'` lies in `A`; the coordinator's cube forms are not in
`V(A,m')`.** (For pair zonotopes this is round 16 Prop 5.7; round 11 `compute.md` Prop 2.2 and round 13 fatpoint
Thm 3.1 are the record's versions.)

### 2.2 Which forms are certified, and why no single one can do the job

The certified space `V_H(A,m')` is large (`dim >= U/2`, t1rigour Thm 2.1(d)), and every element vanishes at the
actual `Z`. The question "find a certified form that is provably nonzero at every nondegenerate point" has a complete
negative answer, for every form of `V(A,m')`, of any height, and every profile in the regime:

**Theorem 2.2 (single-form structural barrier; proved).** Let `S` be a finite subset of `{-1,0,1}^d`, `m >= 1`, and
suppose the profile satisfies

```text
(CG_m)    Lambda(v) > Lambda(S)/m   for every primitive v in {-2,..,2}^d \ 0.
```

Then for every nonzero `c in V(S,m) (x) C`, the zero set of `P_c` in `G_m^d(C)` contains a Zariski-dense subset of a
hypersurface consisting of **nondegenerate** points (`Y^v != 1` for all `v != 0`). In particular this holds for every
`c in V(A,m')`, `m' >= 4w`, for (a) every full-rank actual cluster with `W > 8 ln(2dC')`, and (b) every
gap-admissible fake whose monomial gaps give `X_v >= W/2 - 2 ln(2dC')` for these `v`.

*Proof.* (1) *Classification.* Let `P` be a Laurent polynomial, not a monomial times a constant, all of whose zeros
in `G_m^d(C)` are degenerate. The degenerate locus is `Union_{v != 0} {Y^v = 1}`, a countable union of proper Zariski
closed sets. An irreducible component `H_j` of `V(P)` (a hypersurface) is an irreducible complex variety; it is not
a countable union of proper closed subvarieties (for `d = 1`, `H_j` is a point and degenerate means a root of unity),
so `H_j subset {Y^v = 1} = Union_{zeta^g = 1} {Y^{v'} = zeta}` (`v = g v'`, `v'` primitive) for one `v`, and, being
irreducible of the same dimension, `H_j = {Y^{v'_j} = zeta_j}` (irreducible: complete `v'_j` to a basis). The Laurent
ring is a UFD with units `c Y^a`, and `Y^{v'} - zeta` is irreducible, so

```text
P = c Y^a prod_j (Y^{v'_j} - zeta_j)^{m_j},     zeta_j roots of unity,     ord_e P = sum_{zeta_j = 1} m_j.   (2.3)
```

(2) *Newton polytope.* By Ostrowski, `Newt(P) = a + sum_j m_j [0, v'_j]`. If `P = P_c` with `supp c` in `S` then
`Newt(P)` lies in `[-1,1]^d`, so `sum_j m_j |v'_{j,x}| <= 2` in each coordinate and every `v'_j in {-2..2}^d`. The
vertices of `Newt(P)` carry nonzero coefficients, so `h_{supp c}(beta) = sum_j m_j |<v'_j,beta>|` and
`Lambda(c) = sum_j m_j Lambda(v'_j) >= ord_e(P_c) min_j Lambda(v'_j)`.

(3) *Contradiction.* For `c in V(S,m)`, `ord_e P_c >= m`, and `Lambda(c) <= Lambda(S)`. So `m min Lambda(v'_j) <= Lambda(S)`,
contradicting (CG_m). Hence `P_c` is not of the form (2.3), so some component of `V(P_c)` is not inside a torsion coset
of a proper subtorus, and its nondegenerate points are dense in it (the degenerate ones form a countable union of
proper closed subsets of that component).

(4) *Regime.* `Lambda(A) <= 2w`, `m' >= 4w`, so (CG) needs `Lambda(v) > 1/2`. For a full-rank actual cluster,
`Z^v != 1` for every `v != 0` (`ze.md` Prop 5.1 as corrected by its referee: unique factorisation and affine
independence), and Lemma 2.1 with `|v'|_1 <= 2d` gives `Lambda(v) >= 1 - 4 ln(2dC')/W > 1/2`. For fakes, (CG) is the
stated monomial-gap hypothesis. QED

**Reading.** Every individual certified form, in fact every form of order `> Lambda(A)`, has a dense set of
nondegenerate zeros. So:
* no **single-form** zero estimate, with any constants and any height, can prove `Z notin Bs(V_H)`: it would have to
  use a property of `Z` beyond nondegeneracy (this is profile-uniform and threshold-free, unlike round 26's Thm 3.6,
  which concerned certified *vanishing data at other points* and needed `lambda >= lambda*`);
* in particular, **combinations of cube differences, Combinatorial-Nullstellensatz / Alon-Füredi / Newton grid
  certificates, and symmetrisations over profile automorphisms, when they produce one form, are all excluded**: the
  grid/zonotope ones because they are binomial products (2.3) and cannot fit (§2), the others because Thm 2.2 applies
  to every form;
* a proof must be a **system** argument: it must use many forms of `V_H` at once. §3 gives one.

(The other two suggested tools: Croot-Lev-Pach / slice rank give *upper* bounds for the rank of `(p(k+l))_{k,l}`,
`deg p < m`; the exponential gives the rank-one matrix `(Y^k Y^l)`, so the inequality points the wrong way and yields
nothing. Combinatorial Nullstellensatz with multiplicities (Ball-Serra, Kós-Rónyai) is again a grid statement and
falls under the zonotope cap. These two remarks are short arguments, not theorems with all details.)

## 3. A system-level zero estimate from the translation structure of exponentials (new)

The only symmetry that distinguishes an exponential `f_Y(k) = Y^k` from a polynomial is translation:
`f_Y(k + l) = Y^l f_Y(k)`. A polynomial of degree `e` satisfies `p(k + l) - Y^l p(k) = (1 - Y^l) p_e(k) + (lower)`,
which has full degree `e` when `Y^l != 1`. Exploiting this on the finite set `A`, whose translates overlap only
partially, gives a zero estimate that uses the whole linear system.

**Notation.** For `S` finite in `Z^d` and `l in Z^d`: `S_l := S cap (S - l)` (the `k in S` with `k + l in S`), and
`T_l` the translation of measures, `(T_l c)_k = c_{k-l}`. `L` is a finite set of nonzero shifts. For a field `K` of
characteristic 0, `V(S,e)` is taken over `K`.

**Lemma 3.0 (shift identity; proved).** If `c in V(S_l, e)` then `T_l c - c in V(S, e+1)`, it is supported in `S`,
`|T_l c - c|_inf <= 2|c|_inf`, and

```text
P_{T_l c - c}(Y) = (Y^l - 1) P_c(Y),        Phi^{(e+1)}_{T_l c - c}(u) = (e+1) <l,u> Phi^{(e)}_c(u).      (3.1)
```

*Proof.* `supp c` in `S_l` gives `supp T_l c` in `S_l + l` in `S`. For `|alpha| <= e`,
`sum_k c_k ((k+l)^alpha - k^alpha)` is a combination of moments of `c` of order `<= |alpha| - 1 <= e - 1`, which
vanish; so all moments of order `<= e` of `T_l c - c` vanish. The first identity is
`sum_k c_k Y^{k+l} - sum_k c_k Y^k`. For the second, `<k+l,u>^{e+1} - <k,u>^{e+1} = (e+1)<k,u>^e <l,u> +
sum_{i>=2} binom(e+1,i) <k,u>^{e+1-i} <l,u>^i`, and the terms `i >= 2` are moments of `c` of order `< e`. QED

**Definition (the shift property).** `A` has property `(SHIFT_e)` w.r.t. `L` if

```text
V(A, e)  =  sum_{l in L} V(A_l, e)  +  V(A, e+1),        equivalently   M_e(A) = sum_{l in L} M_e(A_l),     (3.2)
```

equivalently `J(A)_e = intersection_{l in L} J(A_l)_e` (taking leading forms commutes with the intersection of the
ideals `I(A_l)` in degree `e`). The equivalences: `V(S,e)/V(S,e+1) = M_e(S)` via `c -> Phi_c^{(e)}`, and
`M_e(S) = J(S)_e^perp` (record, round 11).

**Theorem 3.1 (multi-shift zero estimate; proved).** Let `A` be finite in `Z^d`, `L` a finite set of nonzero
shifts, `m >= 1`.

1. *(exact)* If `(SHIFT_e)` holds for `e = 0, 1, ..., m-1`, then every `Y in G_m^d(C)` with `Y^l != 1` for all
   `l in L` satisfies `Y notin Bs(V(A,m))`: some `c in V(A,m)` has `P_c(Y) != 0`.
2. *(tangent cone)* Under the same hypothesis, every `u in C^d` with `<l,u> != 0` for all `l in L` has
   `Phi_c^{(m)}(u) != 0` for some `c in V(A,m)`, i.e. `TC(A,m) = V(M_m(A))` is contained in `Union_{l in L} l^perp`.
3. *(height version)* Let `V_H(S,e)` be the `Q`-span of the integer `c in V(S,e)` with `|c|_inf <= H`. If `(SHIFT_e)`
   holds (over `Q`) for `e <= m-2`, and at the top degree

   ```text
   (SHIFT-H_{m-1})     V(A, m-1)  subset  sum_{l in L} V_{H/2}(A_l, m-1)  +  V_H(A, m),                      (3.3)
   ```

   then every `Y` with `Y^l != 1` (`l in L`) satisfies `Y notin Bs(V_H(A,m))`.
4. *(tangent cone with heights)* Under the hypotheses of (3), every `u` with `<l,u> != 0` (`l in L`) has
   `Phi_c^{(m)}(u) != 0` for some integer `c in V(A,m)` with `|c|_inf <= H`, i.e. `u notin TC_H(A,m)`.

*Proof.* (1) We show by induction on `e <= m` that some `c_e in V(A,e)` has `P_{c_e}(Y) != 0`. For `e = 0` take
`c_0 = delta_k`, `k in A` (`P = Y^k != 0`). Given `c_e`, write by (3.2) `c_e = sum_l c'_l + r` with
`c'_l in V(A_l,e)`, `r in V(A,e+1)`. Evaluating, `0 != P_{c_e}(Y) = sum_l P_{c'_l}(Y) + P_r(Y)`. If `P_r(Y) != 0`, put
`c_{e+1} = r`. Otherwise some `P_{c'_l}(Y) != 0`, and `c_{e+1} = T_l c'_l - c'_l in V(A,e+1)` has
`P_{c_{e+1}}(Y) = (Y^l - 1) P_{c'_l}(Y) != 0` by (3.1).
(2) The same induction with `Phi^{(e)}` in place of `P`: `Phi^{(0)}_{delta_k} = 1`; (3.2) in the form
`M_e(A) = sum_l M_e(A_l)` and the second identity of (3.1) give the step.
(3) Part (1) for `m - 1` gives `c in V(A,m-1)` with `P_c(Y) != 0`. By (3.3), `c = sum_l sum_i a_{l,i} g_{l,i} +
sum_i b_i h_i` with integer `g_{l,i} in V(A_l, m-1)`, `|g_{l,i}|_inf <= H/2`, and integer `h_i in V(A,m)`,
`|h_i|_inf <= H`. Some term is nonzero at `Y`: either an `h_i` (which lies in `V_H(A,m)`), or a `g_{l,i}`, and then
`T_l g_{l,i} - g_{l,i}` is an integer vector of `V(A,m)` of height `<= H` with value `(Y^l - 1)P_{g_{l,i}}(Y) != 0`.
(4) As (3), with part (2) for `m - 1`, the map `c -> Phi^{(m-1)}_c` (which kills `V(A,m)`, so the `h_i` terms drop
out: some `g_{l,i}` has `Phi^{(m-1)}_{g_{l,i}}(u) != 0`), and the second identity of (3.1). QED

**Remarks.**
* Nothing about `Y` is used except `Y^l != 1` for `l in L`. For an actual cluster and
  `L_pair := {+-e_x, +-(e_x - e_y)}` this is just **distinctness of the points** (`Z^{e_x - e_y} = z_x/z_y`,
  `Z^{e_x} = z_x/z_n`). No full-rank subcluster, squarefreeness or nondegeneracy hypothesis is needed.
* The criterion is a *linearisation* of the zero estimate: `ZE^all` is a statement about the (nonlinear) base locus of
  a huge linear system; `(SHIFT_e)` is a family of rank equalities `dim sum_l M_e(A_l) = H_A(e)` (or a spanning
  statement), one per degree, about the set `A` alone. It contains no point and no `delta`.
* It is not one of the record's capped single-form tools (Thm 2.2, round 26): the step from `e` to `e+1` uses the
  whole space `V(A_l,e)` (or `V(A,e+1)`), not a fixed form.

### 3.1 What the criterion recovers, and that it is not dominated by product certificates

**Proposition 3.2 (grids; proved).** For the ternary cube `Gamma = {-1,0,1}^d` and `L = {+-e_x}`, `(SHIFT_e)` holds for
all `e < 2d`. So Theorem 3.1 gives `Y notin Bs(V(Gamma, 2d))` whenever all `Y_x != 1`, which is the Newton-interpolation
fact that `Y^k` has degree exactly `2d` on the cube.

*Proof.* `Gamma_{+-e_x} = {k : k_x in {-1,0}}` resp. `{0,1}` in coordinate `x`: grids, so `J(Gamma_{+-e_x}) = (k_x^2) +
(k_y^3 : y)` and `J(Gamma) = (k_y^3 : y)` (leading-form ideal of a grid `prod G_y` is `(k_y^{|G_y|})`). These are
monomial ideals; `k^alpha` lies in every `J(Gamma_{+-e_x})` iff for all `x`, `alpha_x >= 2` or some `alpha_y >= 3`,
i.e. iff `k^alpha in J(Gamma)` or `|alpha| >= 2d`. QED

**Proposition 3.3 (sets where the criterion beats every rational product certificate; exact).**
* *1-D* (`interp_checks/t5_onedim.py`, exact sympy). `S = {0,1,3,10,11,13}`. (a) For `m <= 4`, the gcd of
  `{P_c : c in V(S,m)}` is `(y-1)^m`, so `Bs(V(S,m)) = {1}` and `ZE^all(S,m)` holds; for `m = 5` the gcd has a factor of
  degree 8 whose roots have product of modulus `1008/462 != 1`, so nondegenerate base points exist. (b) In 1-D,
  `M_e(S') = Sym^e` iff `|S'| > e`; since `S_10 = {0,1,3}` and all `|S_l| <= 3`, `(SHIFT_e)` holds exactly for `e <= 2`
  and the criterion certifies `ZE^all(S,3)`. (c) The maximal order at `y = 1` of a **rational** Laurent polynomial
  supported in `S` whose zeros are all roots of unity (equivalently, by (2.3) and Galois closure, `+- y^a prod Phi_k`)
  is **2** (complete enumeration of cyclotomic products of degree `<= 13`).
  So: rational product (zonotope) certificates reach 2, the shift criterion 3, the truth 4.
* *2-D* (`t7_translate2d.py`, `zetaQ.py`; exact over `Q`). `S = S' u (S' + (7,3))` with the Sidon set
  `S' = {(0,5),(1,3),(1,5),(2,2),(3,4),(4,0),(4,5)}`. `(SHIFT_e)` holds over `Q` for `e = 0,1,2` and fails at `e = 3`,
  so the criterion certifies `ZE^all(S,3)` (for `Y` with `Y^l != 1`, `l in S - S`). The complete enumeration of
  rational forms `+- Y^a prod_j Phi_{k_j}(Y^{v_j})` supported in `S` gives maximal order **2**.

(For rational certificates the relevant class is `+- Y^a prod Phi_{k_j}(Y^{v_j})`: by (2.3) a rational form with only
degenerate zeros is `c Y^a prod (Y^{v'} - zeta)^{m}`, and Galois conjugation forces the `zeta`'s attached to each
`v'` to form full orbits. Route 5's certificates are integral, so this is the right comparison class.)

**Reading.** The multi-shift criterion is a genuinely **non-zonotopal** certification mechanism. The record (round 13
§3, round 16 §0) identified "the first non-zonotopal lower-bound certificate at an irrational direction of an
overdetermined box" as exactly the missing tool for route 5. Theorem 3.1 is such a tool *in principle*; the question
is its reach on regime boxes (§4).

### 3.2 Consistency with the known base points, and the provable range

**Proposition 3.4 (the criterion is limited where it must be; proved from the record).** Let `n = 2^k` and take the
Sylvester profile with `k >= 16`. For every admissible order `m' > 2jk` (such orders exist exactly for `k >= 16`,
t1rigour Prop 6.5(c)), `(SHIFT_e)` w.r.t. `L_pair` **fails** for some `e < m'`.

*Proof.* By t1rigour Prop 6.5(b) (record), the `k` coordinate columns have `h_A <= 2j` and a collision-free span `L_F`;
by the cheap-span lemma (`ze.md` §1, record) the subtorus `K_F` lies in `Bs(V(A,m'))` when `m' > 2jk >= rho_A(F)`.
A generic point `Y` of `K_F` has `Y^{e_x - e_y} != 1` and `Y^{e_x} != 1` (collision-free). Theorem 3.1(1) would give
`Y notin Bs(V(A,m'))`. QED

The obstruction has an explicit face: for a family `Q` of sign columns that separates every pair and every point from
the base, `F_Q = prod_{p in Q} k(P_p)^{2j}` lies in `J(A_l)` for every `l in L_pair` (each such `l` shortens the
range of some `k(P_p)`, `p in Q`, to `2j` values), in degree `2j|Q| >= 2j log_2 n`. At the recommended order
`m' = ceil(4w) = 8j` this degree is out of range once `n > 16`, and t1rigour Prop 6.5(a) shows that **every cheap span
lies in the braid arrangement at `m' = ceil(4w)`**: no known base point obstructs `(SHIFT_e)`, `e < ceil(4w)`, w.r.t.
`L_pair`. The larger rational subspaces of the tangent cone found in t1rigour Thm 6.11(b) (`R^{S'}` for a sign column
with side `S'`, `s + j < m'`, at an admissible regime profile, `m' = ceil(4w)`) consist of `u` with `u_x = 0` off `S'`
(`s < m' < d`), so they lie in coordinate hyperplanes `e_x^perp`, `e_x in L_pair`, and do not obstruct either.
Whether a collision-free rational subspace can lie in `TC_H(A, ceil(4w))` is open (t1rigour §6.5, last paragraph);
by Theorem 3.1(4) a positive answer for some regime profile would refute `(SHIFT)` (rational or top-degree height
part) for that profile. (The `F_Q` membership above is an argument at the level of the slab polynomials and is not
used anywhere.)

**Proposition 3.5 (unconditional range; proved).** For every profile and every `T = 1` box, `(SHIFT_e)` holds w.r.t.
`{+-e_x}` for `e <= j - 1`.

*Proof.* *Faithfulness lemma:* if `S` lies in a grid `Gamma = prod G_x` and for every standard monomial `k^alpha` of
`Gamma` (`alpha_x < |G_x|`) of degree `e`, `S` contains a subgrid `prod_{x in supp alpha} G'_x x {b}` with `G'_x` in
`G_x`, `|G'_x| > alpha_x`, then `J(S)_e = J(Gamma)_e`. (Reduce `g in I(S)` of degree `e` modulo the Gröbner basis
`prod_{t in G_x}(k_x - t)`; the top form is reduced modulo the monomial ideal `J(Gamma)`; if a standard monomial
`k^alpha` survives, `g` restricted to the subgrid has `k^alpha` as a top monomial, so by the Combinatorial
Nullstellensatz it is nonzero on the subgrid, a contradiction.) For `S = A`, `Gamma` the cube, and for
`S = A_{+-e_x}`, `Gamma = Gamma_{+-e_x}`, take `b = 0` and `|supp alpha| <= e <= j - 1`: every point `k` of the subgrid
(and `k +- e_x`) has `|<k,beta_p>| <= 2(|supp alpha| + 1) <= 2j <= w`. So `J(A)_e = J(Gamma)_e`,
`J(A_{+-e_x})_e = J(Gamma_{+-e_x})_e`, and Prop 3.2 gives `(SHIFT_e)`. QED

This range (`j`) is below the zonotope range (grids of dimension `j` fit and give order `2j`), so it proves nothing
new about `Z`; it is recorded to show that `(SHIFT)` is not vacuous on regime boxes and to fix where it is open:
**`e in [j, ceil(4w) - 1] = [j, 8j]`**, and the decisive part is `e in (2w(1+o(1)), 4w)`, beyond every zonotope.

## 4. Main result: route 5 reduced to a rank statement about the slab box

**Corollary 4.1 (conditional growth; the implication is proved).** Suppose that for every `n`, every profile with
`n - 1 >= 316 ln 4r` (every regime profile), its `T = 1` box `A` and `m' = ceil(4w)` satisfy

```text
(SHIFT)_A :   M_e(A) = sum_{l in L_pair} M_e(A_l)   for 0 <= e <= m' - 2,   and
              V(A, m'-1)  subset  sum_{l in L_pair} V_{H/2}(A_l, m'-1) + V_H(A, m')   with  H = U^2.
```

Then every cluster of `n` lattice points on an arc of length `C sqrt R` with `W > W_1(n,C,ceil(4w))` has
`n - 1 < 316 ln(4r)`; since actual clusters have `W >= (3 - o(1)) n ln n > W_1` for `n >= n_0(C)`, and `r <= W/ln 5`,

```text
M(C, R)  <=  max( n_0(C), 316 ln(4 log R^2 / ln 5) + 1 )  =  O_C(log log R).
```

(With `H' = e^{m'W/40}` in place of `U^2` the same holds under t1rigour Remark 3.3's condition
`W > 10 d ln 3/m' + 10 ln(e d C'/m')`.)

*Proof.* Let a cluster have `n - 1 >= 316 ln 4r` and `W > W_1`. Its box is a regime box, so `(SHIFT)_A` holds.
`Z^l != 1` for `l in L_pair` (distinct points). Theorem 3.1(3) with `m = m'`, `H = U^2` gives an integer
`c in V(A,m')`, `|c|_inf <= U^2`, with `P_c(Z) != 0`. Rigidity (t1rigour Thm 3.1, record: it holds for any cluster,
any norm, with no normalisation, nondegeneracy or squarefree hypothesis) gives `P_c(Z) = 0`. Contradiction. The size
statement: `W_1(n,C,ceil(4w)) <= 2.3306 sqrt(d/ln 4r) + 8 ln(e sqrt(d/(128 ln 4r))) + 8 ln C'` (t1rigour Cor 3.2) and
`W >= 3n ln n - O(n) - O(n log C')` (`two_thirds/proof.md` Thm 7.5: `3n ln n <= W + (14 + 4 log^+ C) n`). QED

**Quantifiers and constants.** `L_pair` is fixed (`2d + d(d-1)` shifts); `m' = ceil(4w)`; `H = U^2 <= 9^d`; the
hypothesis is required for every regime profile (it is a statement about `A`, which depends only on the column
directions and `r`); no constant depends on the radius; `n_0(C)` is explicit from the two displayed inequalities.
**No template-dependent threshold is treated as uniform**: `(SHIFT)_A` is assumed for *each* profile, not for a
template.

**What the corollary does and does not do.**
* It replaces route 5's missing nonlinear statement (`ZE^all_H`, or ZE at `Z`) by a **linear** one: rank equalities
  `dim sum_l M_e(A_l) = H_A(e)` for `e < m' - 1`, plus a spanning statement in the top degree. Both are about the box
  only, with no point, no `delta`, no height of `Z`.
* The height cut enters only at the top degree, and only *modulo the sum over shifts*: `V_H(A,m')` need not equal
  `V(A,m')`. Round 16 Thm 4.1 gives `codim(V_H(S,e) in V(S,e)) <= r_0 ln|S|/(2 ln H)`; with `|L_pair| ~ d^2` summands
  the top-degree condition asks that the small-height parts of `~d^2` overlapping spaces still span `V(A,m'-1)` modulo
  `V_H(A,m')`. This is a redundancy-versus-codimension statement. It is **not** proved here; it is the H-gap of round
  16/26 in a weaker (relative, top-degree-only) form. I do not claim it dissolves the H-gap.
* The rational part `(SHIFT_e)`, `e < m' - 1`, is **open** at regime size. Prop 3.5 proves it for `e <= j - 1`;
  Prop 3.4 shows it fails for `e` up to `2jk` at large orders on Sylvester boxes (consistently with known base points)
  but nothing obstructs it below `8j`.

## 5. Pre-registered tests T1-T5

**T1 (fake test).** The claims are (a) Thm 2.2, a barrier, and (b) Thm 3.1 / Cor 4.1, a conditional growth
implication. No growth is claimed. For (b): the inputs are (i) rigidity, i.e. exact vanishing of all certified forms
at `Z` (Liouville plus forced divisibility on the whole system), and (ii) `(SHIFT)_A`, a statement about the box
only. Fakes (real angles) violate (i): their `F_c(delta)` do not vanish (t1rigour §3 Remark (ii): `Z_P` is
Lebesgue-null, so almost every fake violates rigidity). They satisfy (ii) or not exactly as actual clusters with the
same profile do, since (ii) depends only on the profile. So Cor 4.1 passes T1 through rigidity, which is the
record's one LP-supercritical, fake-violated input.
*Interaction with the fake-test theorem.* The record's cap (round 12 `fake.md` Thm 2.1, Cor 2.1'; round 16 barrier
Thm 3.5) concerns the quantitative tangent-cone formulation (MIN^pair, `(R_H)`): fakes with directions within
`theta e^{-W/4}` of a nondegenerate real point of `TC_H(A,m')` defeat it. Cor 4.1 uses the **exact** formulation
(`ZE^all_H`), which near-zero fakes do not touch; what would defeat it are nondegenerate **exact** points of
`Bs(V_H(A,m'))` on regime boxes (as in the underdetermined sdc7 model, `fake.md` Cor 2.3). `(SHIFT)_A` excludes
exactly those (with `L_pair`-nondegeneracy). It also excludes exact leading-order barrier points: by Theorem 3.1(4),
`(SHIFT)_A` implies `TC_H(A,m') subset Union_{l in L_pair} l^perp` (the braid arrangement together with the coordinate
hyperplanes). Contrapositively, a regime fake box with a real point `u` of `TC_H(A,m')` having all `u_x` distinct
and nonzero (the round-12/16 notion of a nondegenerate, collision-free barrier point at the exact level) violates
`(SHIFT)_A`. So `(SHIFT)_A` for all regime profiles is **at least as strong as** a negative answer to the exact form
of the barrier question, and is consistent with the fake-test theorem; it does not address barrier points that are
only near-zeros at scale `eps_0`, which the exact route does not need.

**T2 (`k = 4` negative control).** Not applicable: nothing here concerns Question B, `A_k(delta)` or `(L_k^circ)`.

## 6. Open points

1. **`(SHIFT_e)` on regime boxes for `e in (2w(1+o(1)), ceil(4w) - 1)`** (rational, `L_pair`). This is the precise
   statement that would make Theorem 3.1 the first non-zonotopal certificate at regime size. It is a rank equality
   about `A` alone: `dim sum_{l in L_pair} M_e(A cap (A - l)) = H_A(e)`; equivalently "leading forms commute with
   the intersection of the ideals `I(A cap (A-l))`" in degree `e`. Known: true for `e <= j - 1` (Prop 3.5); no known
   base point obstructs it below `8j` (Prop 3.4 discussion); in every computable model it fails exactly where the
   zonotope order and the peak of `H_A` coincide, so the models are uninformative.
   *Strength.* By Theorem 3.1(4), `(SHIFT)_A` (with the top-degree height part) implies
   `TC_H(A, ceil(4w)) subset` (braid arrangement) `u` (coordinate hyperplanes): no collision-free real point of the
   tangent cone. So it is **at least as hard as** the negative answer to round 12's open question (t1rigour §6.5) and
   to the exact form of the round-13/16 barrier question; it is not a shortcut around them. Its advantage is form, not
   strength: it is linear algebra on one explicit set, and it is the kind of statement Gröbner/linkage/standard-monomial
   methods prove.
   It is enough to have `(SHIFT)_A` for the boxes of actual clusters (Cor 4.1 uses only the cluster's own box).
2. **The top-degree height condition** (3.3) with `H = U^2` (or `H' = e^{m'W/40}`): a relative, top-degree-only form of
   the H-gap.
3. **Sharpening the criterion.** Prop 3.3 (1-D) shows a gap between the criterion (3) and the truth (4). Natural
   strengthenings: nested sets `S_0 subset S_1 subset ... subset A` with `V(S_e,e) subset sum_l V(S_{e+1} cap
   (S_{e+1} - l), e) + V(S_{e+1}, e+1)` (the same proof works); the extreme choices are the zonotope chains
   (`S_e + l_e subset S_{e+1}`, capped by Liouville) and the constant chain `S_e = A` (Theorem 3.1). Intermediate chains
   trade the free `(SHIFT)` steps against the `Lambda`-budget (each zonotope step costs `Lambda(l) ~ 1`, `(SHIFT)` steps
   cost nothing).
4. **A linkage reformulation.** By Gorenstein linkage in the cube (round 13 loctheory (A)),
   `H_A(e) = T_d(e) - H_{Gamma \ A}(2d - e)`, and `(SHIFT_e)` becomes a statement about the leading forms of the tail
   set `Gamma \ A` (a union of `r` slab tails) in the top degrees `2d - e`. I did not pursue this; it may be the
   right setting for a proof, since the tails are explicit unions of products of lower sets.
