**What this means for the conjecture.**
* In the (Q) order, where `D` is fixed and the profile is fitted, nothing in the data points to
  growth. The pair channel costs the Perron bound `~ (3-4) M log M` plus a small discrete overhead.
* In the P3-gen order, where `D` comes from the profile's own primes, independent residues force
  `(2 - o(1)) M log^2 M/loglog M` (item 7).
* So a growth proof through pair data needs a statement about **actual** circles: their prime
  residues cannot follow their exponent profile (an `H`-type hypothesis for adapted profiles).
  A construction at `W = O(M log M)` needs the opposite: prime selection that steers residues modulo
  many `q ~ M` at once.
* Both directions remain open. Question (Q) itself is now evidence-only and very likely "yes"; it is
  no longer the bottleneck.
